# Passport Pals: Our Family's World Adventures

A touch-first educational adventure game for Linus (5) and Wesley (3). Fly to seven places the family has visited (South Korea, Mexico City, Japan, Hawaii, Alaska, Costa Rica, Iceland), discover animals, play nine learning mini-games, and collect passport stamps.

Open `index.html` in a browser (iPad/iPhone first). Progress and uploaded photos are saved in the browser on that device. Grown-ups: press and hold "Grown-ups" for 2 seconds.

- `js/data.js` – destinations, animals, words, science questions
- `js/art.js` – drawn art, painted scene hotspots
- `js/games.js` – mini-game library with adaptive difficulty
- `js/app.js` – screens, passport, photos, parent area
- `photos/` – family photos, painted scenes, character cut-outs
